What you’re signing up for

A request for a beta invitation and a subscription to occasional BaseHero news. Neither starts until you confirm your email. Requesting beta access doesn’t guarantee an invitation or create a BaseHero account.

What we keep

Your email address, subscription status and a record of when and how you consented. We use these to manage beta requests and send invitations and newsletters. Cloudflare stores the signup list; Lettermint delivers the emails.

Change your mind any time

Every beta invitation and newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. It stops both kinds of email. You don’t need an account, a password or a reason.
