PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your life. Your work.
Not ours to read.

BaseHero is designed so we can’t see your data. You choose who else can.

End-to-end encrypted.

Your notes, chats and plans are encrypted on your devices before they go anywhere. Nothing we hold can open them.

Private AI, too.

When Hero thinks, searches or works for you in our cloud, it does so on private hardware your device checks first. So we can’t read what you ask it either.

Or bring your own.

Prefer your own AI provider or your own server? Use them instead. Then your data goes to them, not us.

Because it’s private, you can bring more.

Hero is more helpful when it knows the whole story. Bring the things you’d think twice about pasting into an AI chat.

  • A job offer you’re weighing
  • Next quarter’s plans

HELP THAT DOESN’T START OVER EVERY TIME

Hero remembers.
You stay in charge.

Less explaining yourself again. Hero remembers what matters and looks for useful things to do: research to bring back, a loose end to follow up, a draft to get you started.

What it remembers is written down where you can read it, correct it or delete it. And everything it does is there to see and undo, not hidden in a chat you’ve lost track of.
