THE DETAILS

Our privacy promise, in full.

BaseHero is end-to-end encrypted. What you store or share is encrypted on your devices, and nothing we hold can open it, not even all of it together. When Hero or your base’s tools work on it in our cloud, they do so only inside confidential hardware your devices check, so we still can’t read it.

Our private services, Hero’s AI, web search and the work it does in our cloud, run on confidential hardware that your device checks before sending anything, so we can’t read what you send them.

Or bring your own: use your own AI or search provider and it sees what you send it, not us. Host a base yourself and its data and membership stay on your server.
